---
title: "Phase 11: retraining T2 on realistic bearing error does not improve its range"
date: 2026-11-14
---

Phases 7–10 showed that under realistic bearing error (correlated noise plus a drifting bias), the
learned network T2 gives the most robust range estimate. T2 had been trained only on white noise.
The obvious next question: does training it on the realistic error make it better still?

## The test

- **The model.** T2's architecture and configuration, unchanged, trained from scratch on a fresh
  100,000-scenario dataset generated entirely with realistic error. Training took about 16 hours
  on GitHub Actions and stopped early at epoch 48.
- **The gate (G8).** The retrained T2 must:
  - have a median range error wholly below the frozen T2's;
  - pass G5 against the reference IMM.
- **The validation step first.** The six point estimates on the shared validation split must all be
  within their limits before any test data are generated.

## Result: no gain on range, so it stops at validation

| Retrained T2 against | Range                    | Course | Settled course |
| -------------------- | ------------------------ | ------ | -------------- |
| The frozen T2        | **1.021** [0.991, 1.052] | 0.981  | **0.878**      |
| The IMM              | 0.745                    | 0.857  | 1.079          |

- **Range is unchanged.** 12.4% against the frozen T2's 12.2%, and the interval also rules out a
  gain of more than 1%.
- **Settled course improves** by about 12%, mostly on straight legs before any manoeuvre.
- **It still beats the IMM** on overall range and course, and passes G5's settled limits.

## The cost: worse on white noise

On the same scenarios with white noise only, the retrained T2 is clearly worse:

| Median range error | White noise | Realistic error |
| ------------------ | ----------- | --------------- |
| Retrained T2       | 10.3%       | 12.4%           |
| Frozen T2          | **8.0%**    | 12.2%           |
| IMM                | 9.1%        | 16.7%           |

The retrained T2 looks more robust (it degrades 1.21 × against 1.52 ×), but only because it starts
worse. On white noise it loses to the IMM on range.

## Baselines (realistic error, all gated rows)

- **Batch least squares:** 17.1% and 46.0°. It is best before any manoeuvre (6.9%, 2.0°).
- **EKF:** 16.1% and 29.7°. It is best on settled course (9.8°) and speed (1.91 kt).
- **Naive:** 38.7% and 88.3°.
- **Fused tracker and hybrid:** rebuilt on the retrained T2, they are unchanged (within 0.3
  points in range).

## What it means

Seeing the error in training did not buy range accuracy. A plausible reading is that the frozen
T2 is already near what the bearings allow under this error model: phase 9 found the drifting bias
nearly unobservable, and a network cannot learn what the data do not contain.

**Limits:**

- one training run, so the run-to-run spread is unknown;
- one error model at one severity;
- validation data only;
- an architecture that was not re-tuned.

No test data were generated. Phase 10's verdict stands, and the owner decides what comes next.
