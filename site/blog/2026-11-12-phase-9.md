---
title: "Phase 9: a bias-aware IMM cannot see the bias"
date: 2026-11-12
---

Under realistic bearing error (correlated noise plus a drifting bias), the learned range estimate
T2 beat the classical IMM, and no fusion setting could close the gap (phase 8). The natural
classical answer is to model the error: give the IMM a sixth state for the bearing bias and let
it estimate the bias.

## The test

- **The filter:** the reference IMM, plus a drifting bias added to every predicted bearing.
- **Tuning:** the bias's size and time constant and the measurement noise scale, 18 settings in
  all, on validation data with realistic error.
- **The rule, fixed before any setting was scored:** to go to a gate, a setting must beat T2 on
  range and still pass G5 against the plain IMM.
- **The forecast**, recorded beforehand: this would probably fail. A quick check had shown that a
  1° offset barely changes how well a straight-line track fits the bearings.

## Result: it stops at validation

| Bias-state IMM                    | vs plain IMM, range | vs T2, range |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------- | ------------ |
| Best setting (0.25°, 10 min, r 8) | 0.998               | 1.37         |
| Larger bias (1°)                  | 1.05–1.12           | 1.44–1.53    |

- **At best it matches the plain IMM.** It never improves on it.
- **It stays about 1.4 × T2's range error.**
- **Allowing a larger bias makes it worse.** The extra freedom is not pinned down by the bearings.

## Why

From bearings alone, a slowly drifting bias looks almost exactly like a slightly rotated target
track. One or two ownship zigs do not separate the two, so the filter cannot learn the bias. T2
does better on range, plausibly because it learns range from the whole bearing history. That
explanation was not tested.

## Where this leaves things

Phases 7–9 tell one story. Under this error model:

- the learned range is the most robust estimate;
- fusing it into a filter that assumes white noise loses some of that advantage;
- modelling the bias inside the filter does not help, because the bias cannot be observed.

Two options remain for the owner:

- report T2's range directly, as a hybrid;
- close out the project.
