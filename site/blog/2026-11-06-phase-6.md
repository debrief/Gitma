---
title: "Phase 6: the fused tracker's uncertainty, recalibrated, is inconclusive at G7"
date: 2026-11-06
---

C.6's second follow-on asks whether the tracker that passed G5 can say how sure it is. The fused
tracker is the frozen IMM with T2's range fused in.

The owner chose two things:

- **Calibrate the fused tracker's stated uncertainty**, without training.
- **The test: calibrated and sharper.** Its 50% and 90% intervals for range, course and speed must
  cover within ±2 points of nominal, and be narrower than the reference IMM's after the same
  recalibration.

## What was done

- **Stated sds.** Both trackers now write them, from their covariance by the delta method. They
  are badly miscalibrated as stated: typical errors are a fraction of the stated sd, with heavy
  tails.
- **Recalibration, post hoc.** One pair of multipliers per output, fitted on validation, scales
  the sd into 50% and 90% intervals. A two-fold check on validation passed, and decision 33 froze
  the multipliers before seed 215 was generated for scoring.

## Result: inconclusive

| Coverage on seed 215 (G5 rows) | 50%                    | 90%   |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------- | ----- |
| Range (log)                    | **48.3%** [47.8, 48.8] | 89.8% |
| Course                         | 50.5%                  | 90.2% |
| Speed                          | 49.3%                  | 89.6% |

The fused tracker's intervals are narrower than the IMM's: 0.73 × for range, 0.95 × for course and
0.89 × for speed. Eight of the nine checks pass. Range at 50% falls just short: its interval
straddles the 48% floor.

## What the panel added

Five independent reviewers agreed the verdict is right, and reproduced it byte for byte. They also
narrowed what can be claimed:

- **The intervals are calibrated only on the pooled mix of rows.** On quiet straight legs before
  any manoeuvre they are far too wide: range 50% covers 67%. After a manoeuvre they are too narrow:
  38%. One multiplier cannot fix that shape.
- **Course and speed intervals are often uninformative.** 17% of course 90% intervals span the
  whole circle, and 27% of speed 90% intervals reach below zero.
- **The shortfall looks systematic, not noise.** The likely cause is that T2's training epoch, the
  fusion settings and the multipliers were all chosen on the same validation split.
- **The rules were the agent's.** The owner did not get to review them before the decision record.

## What next

The rules allow one re-score on a larger fresh seed. It would probably land near 48.3% again, so a
pass would mean the range coverage just reaches the floor, not that the tracker is well calibrated.
The owner decides whether to run it.
