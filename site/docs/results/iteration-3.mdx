# Results: iteration 3 (second zig, stopped at validation)

**Question:** does a problem with more information in the bearings — a second zig, both turns
across the line of sight (generator 0.3.0, spec 007) — bring the 2c models within the
[G2](iteration-2.mdx) speed threshold? This is the first Part B lever of SRD Annex A (B.2.1),
chosen after the owner's realism check in the [viewer](/viewer).

The models are the 2c configuration, trained from scratch on seed 204. A pre-registered validation
rule (spec 007 FR-006) decides whether the pair goes to seed 204's test split: the LSTM's G2 speed
interval must reach below 1 kt.

:::warning Stopped at validation: speed did not reach the threshold
**The LSTM is at 1.10 kt [1.06, 1.16]** at zig + 600 s on the σ = 1° slice, against 1 kt, so the
rule stops the iteration and the test split is not scored. The forecast recorded before training
was 1.1–1.3 kt.

**Classical gained more.** It reaches 0.34 kt on the same rows; the LSTM is 3.3 × classical on
speed, against 2.4 × in iteration 2c.

**The BiLSTM is the closest any model has come:** 1.02 kt [0.98, 1.05], reported only. Range and
course pass with the largest margins of the project.

## Seed-204 validation split (σ = 1°)

| Estimator | Range, zig + 300 s | Course, zig + 300 s | Speed, zig + 600 s |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| G2 threshold | < 20% | < 10° | < 1 kt |
| lstm-instantaneous (iteration 3) | **8.8%** [8.5, 9.1] | **6.19°** [5.89, 6.41] | 1.10 kt [1.06, 1.16] |
| bilstm-time-late (iteration 3) | **7.2%** [7.0, 7.5] | **4.36°** [4.18, 4.54] | 1.02 kt [0.98, 1.05] |
| lstm-instantaneous (2c, reported) | 11.4% | 6.53° | 2.01 kt |
| bilstm-time-late (2c, reported) | 17.7% | 7.72° | 1.93 kt |
| classical | 3.4% | 1.43° | 0.34 kt |
| naive | 40.2% | 89.7° | 4.23 kt |

n = 4,883 scenarios at zig + 300 s (99% of the slice) and 3,165 at zig + 600 s (64%). The 2c
models were trained on the two-leg problem and are out of distribution here.

Zig + 300 s falls mostly during the second turn, so it is not like-for-like with iterations 1–2c;
by zig + 600 s nearly every scenario is on leg 3 (spec 007, "Reading the gate under three legs").
