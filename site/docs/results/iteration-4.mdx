# Results: iteration 4 (velocity outputs and derived features, G2 passed)

**Question:** do velocity outputs (vx, vy) and the derived feature set together bring the models
within the [G2](iteration-2.mdx) speed threshold on [iteration 3](iteration-3.mdx)'s three-leg problem?
These are Annex A levers B.2.2 and B.2.3, chosen by the owner (decision 23, spec 008).

:::tip G2 passed on the re-score
**On seed 205's test split, G2 was inconclusive.** Instantaneous speed was 0.981 kt
[0.940, 1.031]; every other check passed.

**SRD §7 allows one re-score** on a larger fresh-seed test set, where a second inconclusive fails.
It was pre-registered as seed 206 with 100,000 scenarios and the same frozen models. **The re-score
passes every check.**

- The instantaneous LSTM reaches **0.939 kt [0.926, 0.951]** at zig + 600 s, against 1 kt.
- The BiLSTM reaches 0.740 kt.
- Range and course pass for both models with wide margins.

**The models are still about 3 × classical on speed** (0.319 kt on the same rows). G2 ("useful") is
passed; G3 (classical parity) is far off.

Records: the
[re-score](https://github.com/debrief/Gitma/tree/main/experiments/20261025-i4-g2-rescore) (its

**Rows used:**
- **At zig + 300 s:** n = 49,574 scenarios (500 excluded; 99% of the slice). At this point the
  rows are mostly in the second turn.
- **At zig + 600 s:** n = 32,266 (17,808 excluded; 64% of the slice). Of these rows, 32,228 are
  on leg 3.

**Other checks:**
- **G1 ratios** (model over naive range error) are 0.207 and 0.152, both passing.
- **Time-late not worse** than instantaneous holds at all 21 curve points.
- **Shuffled labels** score at naive (0.984 ×).
- **Both controls** are beaten.

## Seed 205 and seed 206

The two results are not pooled, as pre-registered.

| Split | n at zig + 600 s | LSTM speed | BiLSTM speed | Classical |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| seed-205 validation | 3,147 | 0.94 kt [0.91, 0.98] | 0.77 kt | 0.31 kt |
| seed-205 test (G2 inconclusive) | 3,250 | 0.981 kt [0.940, 1.031] | 0.754 kt | 0.314 kt |
| **seed-206 test (G2 pass)** | 32,266 | **0.939 kt [0.926, 0.951]** | 0.740 kt | 0.319 kt |

**Why the two test points differ.** Seed 206's point lies just below seed 205's interval: a gap of
0.042 kt, z ≈ 1.8, two-sided p ≈ 0.07.

- **Mix.** Reweighting seed 206 to seed 205's mix of target speeds and ranges explains about
  0.006 kt.
- **Chance.** The rest is chance: seed 205's test draw was high, and its own validation split gave
  0.94.

**The speed bias is the frozen model's.** It was forecast before the re-score.
