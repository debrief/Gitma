# Results: iteration 2c (G2, final attempt)

**Question:** do the two levers that each moved speed on validation reach the
[G2](iteration-2.mdx) speed threshold together? Iteration 2c (spec 005) combines the speed-weighted
loss of [iteration 2b](iteration-2b.mdx) with the capacity the scan pointed to (two stacked
128-unit recurrent layers, from scratch) and judges G2 on a third fresh test split (seed 203). It
is the last iteration the SRD allows under G2 (§7, amended 2026-10-15 before 2b's test scoring).

The carried set is the 2c models, declared before training; the 2b models are scored beside them on
the same split and reported, never gated. Thresholds, slice, coverage and the pass / fail /
inconclusive rule are as on the [iteration-2 page](iteration-2.mdx).

:::danger G2 failed; the project stops at G2 (agent review panel, round 2)
**Speed fails for both models, by less again.** The instantaneous model is at 1.47 kt
[1.37, 1.52] and the time-late model at 1.53 kt [1.47, 1.61], against 1 kt; the 2b models on the
same scenarios are at 1.78 kt and 1.77 kt, and classical at 0.61 kt, at the
[Cramér–Rao bound](../glossary.md).

Range and course are the best of the project and pass for both models: 13.5% / 7.36° instantaneous
and 12.1% / 6.12° time-late. The time-late model is now better than the instantaneous one on range
and course, and worse on speed.
