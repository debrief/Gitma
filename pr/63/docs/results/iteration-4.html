Results: iteration 4 (velocity outputs and derived features, G2 passed)

Question: do velocity outputs (vx, vy) and the derived feature set together bring the models within the G2 speed threshold on iteration 3's three-leg problem? These are Annex A levers B.2.2 and B.2.3, chosen by the owner (decision 23, spec 008).

G2 passed on the re-score

On seed 205's test split, G2 was inconclusive. Instantaneous speed was 0.981 kt [0.940, 1.031]; every other check passed.

SRD §7 allows one re-score on a larger fresh-seed test set, where a second inconclusive fails. It was pre-registered as seed 206 with 100,000 scenarios and the same frozen models. The re-score passes every check.

  • The instantaneous LSTM reaches 0.939 kt [0.926, 0.951] at zig + 600 s, against 1 kt.
  • The BiLSTM reaches 0.740 kt.
  • Range and course pass for both models with wide margins.

The models are still about 3 × classical on speed (0.319 kt on the same rows). G2 ("useful") is passed; G3 (classical parity) is far off.

Records: the re-score (its review.md holds the run history and the panel), the seed-205 gate, the re-score decision, and the write-up.

Why the two test points differ. Seed 206's point lies just below seed 205's interval: a gap of 0.042 kt, z ≈ 1.8, two-sided p ≈ 0.07.

  • Mix. Reweighting seed 206 to seed 205's mix of target speeds and ranges explains about 0.006 kt.
  • Chance. The rest is chance: seed 205's test draw was high, and its own validation split gave 0.94.

The speed bias is the frozen model's. It was forecast before the re-score.
