Scenario generator

Source: packages/sim/src/scenario.ts (generator version 0.2.0). Requirements: SRD §2–3, spec 001 FR-001–FR-009, spec 003 FR-001–FR-006 (provenance and the rate-limited turn).

Frame and units​

Local flat-earth Cartesian: x east, y north, metres, origin at ownship's position at window start. Bearings and courses are true, clockwise from north, radians in [0, 2π). Speeds in m/s; times in seconds since window start. Every stored value is rounded to float32 (negative zero stored as +0).

Random numbers​

Each scenario has its own seed, seed_i = SplitMix64(dataset_seed) output number i. The scenario's stream is xoshiro128** seeded from two SplitMix64 outputs of seed_i. Uniform doubles use 53 bits from two 32-bit outputs; Gaussian draws use Box–Muller (one variate per draw).

Draw order​

For each attempt: sample count S (uniform integer, window/dt), ownship speed, ownship course c₀, |zig angle|, zig sign (±), zig fraction f, initial range r₀ (log-uniform by default), initial bearing β₀, target speed v_t, target course θ_t, noise σ (uniform choice from a set), dropout fraction d, then — only when the ranges give ownship_turn_rate_rad_s — the turn rate ω (uniform; iteration 2 uses 0.5–3°/s). Generator 0.3.0 (iteration 3) then draws, only when the ranges ask for them:

  • Across-the-line-of-sight offset. The first turn's offset, from los_offset_rad, under zig_rule: across_los.
  • Second zig. Its delay, from zig2_delay_s, then its offset (or, under the random rule, its size and side).

Generator 0.4.0 (phase 3, spec 010) then draws, only when the ranges give target_manoeuvre_fraction, four values for every scenario, whether or not its target turns:

  • Whether the target turns. A uniform draw below the fraction (0.8 in phase 3).
  • Its course change. Signed, |angle| from target_manoeuvre_angle_abs_rad (30–120°).
  • Its turn rate. From target_turn_rate_rad_s (0.5–3°/s).
  • When. A whole start sample, uniform from target_manoeuvre_after_zig_s (2 min) after zig_index to the last start that ends the turn target_manoeuvre_margin_s (6 min) before the window ends.

A scenario whose window cannot fit that is redrawn, whether or not its target turns, so turning and held-course targets share one window distribution. The constraint does shift it: about 0.6 redraws per accepted scenario on the phase-3 ranges (0.21 of them already there before the target's turn): windows under about 1,100 s all but disappear (P10 1,360 s against 990 s), and the realised angles and rates lean slightly towards short turns. Phase-3 figures are therefore not comparable with earlier iterations' without re-running them on phase-3 data.

All parameters are float32-rounded before use, so the metadata describes the data exactly. Ranges without a turn rate consume the random stream exactly as generator 0.1.0 did, ranges without the 0.3.0 options exactly as 0.2.0 did, and ranges without a target manoeuvre exactly as 0.3.0 did. Each reproduces earlier datasets byte for byte.

Motion​

Zig sample k = round(f · S), zig time t_z = k · dt, leg-2 course c₁ = c₀ + zig.

Instantaneous zig (no turn rate; iterations 0–1)​

Ownship at sample i (time t = i · dt), speed v:

  • i < k: p(t) = v t (sin c₀, cos c₀)
  • i ≥ k: p(t) = v t_z (sin c₀, cos c₀) + v (t − t_z)(sin c₁, cos c₁)

zig_index = k.

Rate-limited turn (iteration 2)​

A real ship cannot change course instantly (SRD §3: "instantaneous in iteration 1, then rate-limited"). With turn rate ω, ownship keeps speed v and turns towards the zig side at a constant rate starting at t_z, from the leg-1 point p_z = v t_z (sin c₀, cos c₀). The turn lasts T = |zig| / ω. With the signed rate ω_s = sign(zig) · ω (positive = clockwise, to starboard) and τ = t − t_z:

  • course c(τ) = c₀ + ω_s τ
  • position on the arc (closed form; centre |v/ω| abeam on the turn side): p = p_z + (v / ω_s) · (cos c₀ − cos c(τ), sin c(τ) − sin c₀)

So ownship is on leg 1 for i ≤ k, on the arc while τ < T, and on leg 2 from the arc's end point p_T afterwards: p = p_T + v (τ − T)(sin c₁, cos c₁). Worked check: heading north at 5 m/s, a 90° turn at 1°/s ends 90 s later at (r, r) from its start, r = v/ω ≈ 286.5 m, on course 90°.

Metadata: turn_start_index = k + 1 (the first sample after the turn starts) and zig_index = the first sample with τ ≥ T, i.e. the first sample on leg 2. Everything downstream that uses zig_index (loss masking, "zig + 300 s" gate points, curves) therefore measures time on the new leg, as in iteration 1. A turn that would not finish inside the window is redrawn (it cannot happen with the iteration-2 ranges: at most 180 s of turn against at least 240 s after the turn starts).

Across the line of sight (iteration 3)​

Under zig_rule: across_los each turn is chosen from the geometry at its start, not drawn. Take ownship at p on course c and the true bearing b to the target at the last sample before the turn. The relative bearing is ρ = b − c, wrapped to (−180°, 180°].

  • New course. The turn puts the target on the other bow at the drawn offset φ (30–90° in iteration 3): the new course is b + sign(ρ) · φ.

  • Turn direction. The turn takes the shorter way, so zig = wrap(b + sign(ρ) φ − c) and |zig| ≤ 180°.

  • Through the bow line of sight. When |ρ| + φ < 180° the turn is towards the target.

  • Through the stern line of sight. Otherwise the shorter turn is away from the target.

Either way, ownship's velocity component across the line of sight reverses, which is what makes the bearings informative (spec 007 research R4). The rule holds at the start of the turn. During a long turn at short range the bearing keeps drawing, so on the new leg the angle off can fall outside the drawn range, and in rare cases the target returns to the original bow. Turns near 180° can go either way for a tiny change in geometry. The realised turn is stored as zig_angle_rad, so the tracks depend only on the parameters.

Worked check: heading north with the target 40° on the starboard bow and φ = 60°, the turn is 100° to starboard. With the target on the starboard quarter (150°) it is 150° to port.

Second zig (iteration 3)​

With zig2_delay_s the second turn starts at sample k₂ = zig_index + round(delay / dt), still on the leg-2 course. It follows the first turn's conventions:

  • Turn. It is instantaneous at k₂, or an arc at the same turn rate.
  • Indices. turn2_start_index = k₂ + 1, and zig2_index is the first sample on leg 3.
  • Redraws. A second turn that does not finish inside the window is redrawn. Gate times still count from zig_index, the end of the first turn.

Target: q(t) = r₀ (sin β₀, cos β₀) + v_t t (sin θ_t, cos θ_t).

If the range |q − p| falls below min_range_m (100 m) at any sample, the parameters are redrawn from the same stream; the redraw count is recorded in the dataset header.

Observations​

For each sample: ε ~ N(0, σ²) and u ~ U[0, 1) are drawn (always both, so the stream does not depend on outcomes). The bearing is wrap(atan2(q_x − p_x, q_y − p_y) + ε). If u < d the sample is a dropout: valid = false, bearing stored as 0.

Provenance​

Every dataset header records the code that generated it: generator_git_commit (the checkout's commit) and generator_git_dirty (uncommitted changes, untracked files included). tma-generate refuses a dirty tree, or a directory outside git, unless --allow-dirty is given; the header then says generator_git_dirty: true (commit "unknown" outside git).

The header travels with the data: features files (format 2) embed the source header and the dataset file's SHA-256, and tma-train refuses features whose lineage is missing, dirty, from a generator that differs from the commit at which G0 was verified, from different datasets for train and validation, or built for another feature set. The model manifest records the source header and the G0 experiment the training guard accepted. See feature layout.

Checks​

Hand-computed known answers (packages/sim/test/unit/known-answer.test.ts, and for the turn turn.test.ts) check the frame independently of the classical solver, so a sign or frame error shared by generator and solver cannot pass unnoticed. G0's independent-geometry check includes an in-turn case: the bearing half-way through a 60° turn at 1°/s, computed with literal Math.atan2 from the closed-form arc.

Target course change (generator 0.4.0)​

The target holds course and speed v_t to the start sample k_m, then turns at the drawn rate ω_m on a constant-radius arc (arcPosition, as ownship's turns) for |Δθ| / ω_m seconds, then holds the new course θ_t + Δθ at the same speed. Metadata manoeuvre_start_index is k_m + 1 (the first sample after the turn starts) and manoeuvre_index the first sample after it ends; both are absent when the target holds course. Gate times for G4 count from manoeuvre_index.

Ownship's zigs under zig_rule: across_los are planned on the target's straight track, as an operator plans on the current solution. After the target turns, a later zig need not put the actual target across the line of sight; G0 checks the rule against the straight track.
