shared brief listed the evidence: this folder, decision 31, the research folder, Annex C §C.11,

### What the reviewers verified

- **Geometry.**
  - The log-range model is correct: H = [dx/ρ², dy/ρ²], orthogonal to the bearing Jacobian.
  - Ownship is taken at the same sample, and T2's `range_m` is the lognormal median, consistent
    with h = ln ρ.
  - The update is gated to i ≥ zig_index + 1.
  - The slice semantics match g5.md, and the ratios re-derived from `report.json` agree.
- **Estimation.**
  - The update is a correct EKF update (Joseph form, symmetrised, innovation ln z − ln ρ).
  - Its order within the IMM is correct.
  - `fusedPredict`, a copy of the research script and the scored file agree exactly on 300 test
    scenarios (38,069 rows; maximum difference 0).
  - Numerical hazards end in re-initialisation through `plausible`.
- **Statistics.**
  - The rows, the clustered paired bootstrap (2,000 resamples, 97.5%) and the FR-015 rule were
    checked against the code.
  - Overall course was recomputed independently: 0.947 [0.936, 0.960], an exact match.
  - Intervals were computed for every sub-slice and cross-estimator claim.
- **Reproducibility.**
  - The git order and the file times both follow the decision.
  - The plan's digests and all 8 file digests match.
  - The built `dist/` is byte-identical to the source compiled at HEAD.
  - The fused file (c085ec22…) and the IMM file (f6d8a504…) were regenerated byte-identical.
  - `verdict.json` and `report.json` were re-run byte-identical.
  - The gate refuses a broken prediction chain.
- **Red team.**
  - No leakage path: T2 is causal and normalised on inputs only, and the fusion reads T2's row at
    sample i only.
  - No sign that seed 214 was scored before the decision.
  - The fused file was rebuilt byte-identical.
  - The bindings were exercised.
