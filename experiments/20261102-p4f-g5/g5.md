## What the pass does not show

- **Before any manoeuvre the fusion did not beat the IMM on test.** Its range there is 1.03 × the
  IMM's (4.16% against 4.04%), against 0.94 × on validation. That is close to parity and far better
  than the hybrid's 1.43 ×. But "it also wins on clean geometry" did not transfer. The gains come
  from rows after a manoeuvre (11.1% against 15.1%).
- **On straight legs, the EKF and batch least squares are still better.**
  - Settled before any manoeuvre, the EKF has 3.59% range and 1.88° course; least squares has
    2.58% and 0.72°.
  - Against the EKF the fused tracker would fail settled course (1.41 ×). Settled speed would be
    1.07 × and settled range 0.92 ×.
  - The IMM reference was pre-registered (decision 29, chosen on overall range), so the gate
    stands. But "best on straight legs" is not shown by any learned combination so far.
- **Against the hybrid** (point estimates on the same rows):
  - the fused tracker is better on overall range (0.824 against 0.864), settled range (0.864
    against 0.991), settled course (0.948 against 0.982) and settled speed (0.897 against 0.999);
  - it is worse on overall course (0.947 against 0.911) and on range after a manoeuvre;
  - the hybrid has the larger smallest margin over the five checks (0.089 against 0.053, from
    `nees.json`'s script).
- **Settled course missed its forecast band:** 0.948 against a forecast of 0.87–0.94. Validation was
  0.908. Every other check fell inside its band.
- **The covariance is not calibrated** (`nees.json`, 4-state NEES on the G5 rows):

  | | Mean | Median | Within 95% bound | Settled: mean | median | within 95% |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Fused | 273 | 1.10 | 94.1% | 141 | 0.46 | 97.7% |
  | IMM | 1,857 | 0.98 | 92.7% | 154 | 0.36 | 96.4% |

  The covariance is too wide on typical rows and has an overconfident tail. The fusion shortens the
  tail and does not make it worse.
- **Rows with no converged estimate count as infinite errors**, as in phase 4b. There are 127 for
  the fused tracker, 145 for the IMM and 91 for the EKF (0.2% of 67,403); the effect on the medians
  is negligible.
