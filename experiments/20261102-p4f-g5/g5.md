# Phase 4c G5 on seed 214 (spec 014, decision 31)

**Judged:** the fused tracker `fused-lstm-gaussian-imm-cv0.01-ct0.03-w0.01-s0.001-p4-r8-k4-m6`.

- It is the frozen reference IMM with the frozen T2's range fused in as a log-range measurement.
- The measurement's sd is 4 × T2's stated log-range sd, applied every 6 samples after the bearing
  update.
- It carries one state, so range, course and speed come from the same filter. That state takes a
  second estimator's range, which saw the same bearings; k and M are an empirical correction for
  that, not a model of it.
- It is reported as a filter combined with a frozen network, never as T2 alone.

**Reference:** the frozen IMM `imm-cv0.01-ct0.03-w0.01-s0.001-p4-r8` (decision 29).

**Data:** seed 214's 40,000-scenario test split, generated alone at a clean commit after decision 31
and pinned in `configs/phase4/f/plan.json` (content 6f63adca…). G5 rows: 67,403, of which 29,981
are settled.

**Commands:** `run.sh`. File digests: `predictions.sha256`.

## What the pass does not show

Intervals in this section are paired, scenario-clustered 97.5% bootstrap intervals on the same rows.
They were computed by the statistics reviewer (panel round 1) and are reported, not gated.

- **Before any target manoeuvre it only matches the IMM.**
  - On settled rows before any manoeuvre, fused/IMM is: range 1.029 [0.994, 1.068], course 1.007
    [0.987, 1.031], speed 0.984 [0.949, 1.012]. At P90 it is better (range 14.7% against 17.2%).
  - On validation the median range ratio was 0.94. That did not transfer. The ratio is sensitive to
    k and M (0.88–1.19 across neighbouring variants), so the validation value was optimistic. The
    test interval includes parity.
  - It is far better than the hybrid's 1.43 ×.
  - The gains come after manoeuvres: settled after a manoeuvre, range is 0.738 [0.717, 0.759].
- **Before any target manoeuvre, the EKF and batch least squares are better.**
  - There the EKF has 3.59% range and 1.88° course; least squares has 2.58% and 0.72°.
  - On all settled rows, fused/EKF is: course 1.410 [1.360, 1.454], speed 1.074 [1.044, 1.105], range
    0.917 [0.894, 0.942]. Against the EKF it would fail settled course.
  - Overall it beats the EKF clearly: range 0.802 [0.787, 0.816], course 0.779 [0.766, 0.791].
  - After a manoeuvre the EKF (16.8%) and least squares (32.8%) are far worse than the fused tracker
    (11.1%).
  - The IMM reference was pre-registered (decision 29, chosen on overall range), so the gate stands.
- **Against the phase-4b hybrid** (fused/hybrid):
  - better on overall range 0.954 [0.941, 0.968], settled range 0.872, settled course 0.965 and
    settled speed 0.898, each interval wholly below 1;
  - **worse on overall course: 1.040 [1.025, 1.056]**;
  - smallest margin over the five checks, using point estimates against the limits (from `nees.json`'s
    script): fused 0.053, hybrid 0.089.
- **The variant search was larger than decision 31 says.** The decision, written before scoring,
  says k and M were chosen from 12 variants. In fact 18 were scored, adaptively, in two stages. It
  also described a plateau in u = M·k², which the grid does not support. Both are corrected in
  `research.md` and spec 014. The fresh seed controls the test either way.
- **Settled course missed its forecast band** (0.948 against 0.87–0.94).
  - The band was narrower than validation's own interval, [0.873, 0.952].
  - Decision 31 argued that k and M would transfer because phase 4b's θ-dependent ratios moved by
    less than 0.01. But every fused ratio depends on k, M and T2's range, and phase 4b's
    range-dependent ratios moved by +0.015 and +0.051.
  - Estimators that k and M could not affect (the hybrid, T2) moved towards 1 by similar amounts
    between seeds 211 and 214. So most of the validation-to-test shift is a population effect, and
    only settled course suggests extra selection optimism (about 0.03–0.04).
- **The covariance is not calibrated** (`nees.json`, 4-state NEES on the G5 rows; non-converged rows
  are dropped, so each estimator's row set differs slightly):

  | | Median | Above the 95% bound (9.49) | Settled: median | above 9.49 |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Fused | 1.10 | 5.9% | 0.46 | 2.3% |
  | IMM | 0.98 | 7.3% | 0.36 | 3.6% |

  Both covariances are too wide on typical rows (3.36 is the honest median) and have an
  overconfident tail. The means (273 and 1,857) are dominated by a few rows and are not used.
  Against the nominal bounds the fusion is no worse. But its settled median rose while its error
  fell, so the covariance shrank faster than the error. The reference's inflation could mask double
  counting.
- **What the sequence of passes supports.**
  - Phases 4b and 4c each judged a new estimator at G5 on its own fresh, pre-committed seed.
  - The designs were chosen one after another, after seeing earlier results, on the same validation
    split.
  - Both reuse the same frozen T2 and the same IMM, so they are not independent replications. They
    show that T2's range, switched in or fused, helps this IMM; they do not show that a learned
    tracker alone does.
  - T2 alone still fails on this seed: settled course 1.495 ×, settled speed 1.655 ×.
  - A Bonferroni correction over the three G5 attempts would need about 99.2% intervals. The tightest
    upper bound (overall course) would then be about 0.961, still below 1.
- **Rows with no converged estimate count as infinite errors**, as in phase 4b. There are 127 for
  the fused tracker, 145 for the IMM, 91 for the EKF and 3.9% of least squares' rows (0.2% of
  67,403 for the trackers); the effect on the medians is negligible.

## One track state (`bearing-rate.json`)

| G5 rows | Fused | IMM | Hybrid | T2 | EKF |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Implied bearing-rate error, median (°/min), settled | 0.100 | 0.103 | 0.127 | 0.143 | 0.079 |
| Bearing error after 5 min dead reckoning, median, settled | 1.02° | 1.06° | 1.49° | 1.57° | 0.92° |

Both measures are taken against the true, noise-free geometry, not the measured bearings. So they
show that the fused state's implied bearing rate is as accurate as the IMM's; the hybrid's mixed
state is about 0.5° further off after 5 min.
- That the fused tracker is one track state follows from the code: a single `ImmState`. This check
  is necessary, not sufficient.
- Each estimator drops its own non-converged rows, and 0.100 against 0.103 has no interval, so
  "as accurate as" is the claimed strength.
- Dead reckoning on settled rows can cross a later manoeuvre. That affects every estimator alike.
